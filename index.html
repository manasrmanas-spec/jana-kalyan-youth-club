About Jana Kalyan Youth Club

Jana Kalyan Youth Club is a social organization dedicated to serving society, helping needy people, organizing blood donation camps, environmental awareness programs, cultural activities, and youth development initiatives.

Our mission is to build unity, leadership, discipline, and a better future through community service.
